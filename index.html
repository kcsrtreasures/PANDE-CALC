Starter amount is calculated from total flour multiplied by your selected starter percentage.

Important: The percentage is starter weight divided by total recipe flour. The initial suggestions are 20% for same-day fermentation and 10% for overnight refrigeration. These are starting estimates, not guaranteed fermentation formulas. Actual timing depends on starter activity, dough temperature, room temperature and recipe composition.
